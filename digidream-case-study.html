The Work

SEO for the agency itself

Digidream.net is a digital marketing agency that needed to practice what it preaches — their own site wasn't matching the search visibility you'd expect from an agency selling SEO services.

I ran a full technical audit, fixed crawlability and indexing issues, overhauled on-page structure across key service pages, and built a content strategy around high-intent keywords. The result was compounding organic growth that the GSC data below shows clearly.
