The Work

Local SEO from the ground up

Scotsdale had a weak local footprint — low Google Business Profile engagement and barely visible in local pack results across Scottsdale. The brief was to change that through a systematic Local SEO approach.

I audited and fully optimised the GBP listing, built out location-specific content, cleaned up citations, and drove a consistent review acquisition strategy. Local rank tracking with a grid-based tool confirmed position movement across the whole area — not just the immediate neighbourhood.
